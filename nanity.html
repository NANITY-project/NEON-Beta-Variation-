03 — Model Compatibility

One architecture.
Total agreement.

NEON does not guess at architecture — it runs one fixed contract. Any GGUF declaring general.architecture = "nanity" and matching the spec below loads; nothing else is recognized. This is what NEON's forward pass, tokenizer, and quantization layer currently support.
